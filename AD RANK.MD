In Google Ads, **Ad Rank** is the metric Google uses to determine your ad's position (or if it appears at all). When the Ad Preview tool says **"Your ad has a low Ad Rank for this search,"** it means your combination of **Bid** and **Ad Quality** is not high enough to win a placement for that specific search query.

Here are the 5 core factors behind a low Ad Rank:

### 1. Quality Score (The biggest impact)
Quality Score (rated 1 to 10) determines your ad relevance and price. It has three components:
* **Expected CTR:** The likelihood that users will click your ad. If your ad copy is generic, expected clicks will drop, lowering Ad Rank.
* **Ad Relevance:** How closely your ad headline/description matches the search query. For example, if a user searches for *"kidney stone laser treatment Patna"* and your ad copy says *"General Surgery Hospital,"* Google deems it irrelevant. 
* **Landing Page Experience:** How relevant and fast your website landing page is. If they click a kidney stone ad and land on a generic homepage, or the page loads slowly, your Ad Rank suffers.

### 2. Your Maximum CPC Bid
* If your maximum bid (what you are willing to pay for a click) is set lower than competitors or below the minimum bid required for that keyword's search volume, your ad will not show.

### 3. Missing Ad Assets (Extensions)
* Google calculates Ad Rank by factoring in the expected impact of your assets (e.g., Sitelinks, Callouts, Call Assets, Structured Snippets). 
* **Action:** Ensure you have configured **Call Assets** (using your primary number `07250520694`) and sitelinks for departments.

### 4. Search Context
* Ad Rank is calculated in real-time for every single search. It changes based on the user's **device** (mobile vs. desktop), **location** (e.g., if they are outside your targeted Patna radius), **time of day**, and search terms.

### 5. Ad Rank Thresholds
* Google sets minimum quality thresholds. If your bid is high but your quality is extremely low, you cannot bypass the threshold, and your ad will be suppressed.
